Sign in to scan your tenant

You'll grant read-only access to directory, apps, policy, sign-in logs and GSA configuration. Nothing is stored — analysis runs in your browser.

Passbeck Discovery is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Microsoft. You sign in with your own Microsoft account and grant read-only access. Your tenant data is read directly from Microsoft Graph by your browser and analysed locally — Passbeck receives none of it and stores nothing on our side. The client-side source is public, so you can verify this yourself.

How to revoke: Entra admin center → Identity → Applications → Enterprise applications → "Passbeck Discovery" → Remove. Or revoke from myaccount.microsoft.com.

No consent to Passbeck? Use your own Graph connection

Regulated buyers can run the same scan locally with their own Microsoft Graph credentials and upload the result — no consent to Passbeck required. The script still needs its own read-only Graph permissions.
