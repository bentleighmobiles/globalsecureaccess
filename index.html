The shift

Your VPN is a liability, not a strategy.

A traditional VPN often grants broader access than each user actually needs — one tunnel, many reachable resources. Microsoft's answer — Global Secure Access — replaces that with per-app, identity-based Zero Trust: users authenticate to each app, and access follows identity, device posture and risk — not the network they're on. Not every app forces a fresh interactive login; single sign-on and conditional access carry the user through.

But buying the license is only step one. Where GSA projects stall is deployment: app discovery, connector rollout, Conditional Access migration, and change management. That's the part we do every day.
